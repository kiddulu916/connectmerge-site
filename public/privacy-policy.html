Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Connect Merge collects, why, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Game data. Puzzle progress, scores, and streak history are stored locally on your device and synced to our backend to power leaderboards and cross-device continuity.
  • Contact hashes. With your permission, we read your device contacts and store only a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of each phone number — never the raw number or name — to discover which contacts also play Connect Merge.
  • Advertising identifiers. Google Mobile Ads may collect your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • Local notifications. If you grant permission, we send daily reminders. No notification data leaves your device.
  • Diagnostic data. Basic crash and error information (OS version, app version) may be collected to fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Display global and friends leaderboards.
  • Restore your progress when you reinstall or switch devices.
  • Match you with friends who also play (via hashed contacts only).
  • Show advertising to keep the app free.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the game.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase — database and auth infrastructure.
  • Google Mobile Ads — advertising network. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

Server-side data (scores, hashed contacts) is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us or using the Delete My Data page.

5. Children's Privacy

Connect Merge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. You can revoke contacts permission at any time in your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The effective date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
